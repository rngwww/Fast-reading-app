How TACHYON RSVP Works

1

Fixate on the Optimal Recognition Point (ORP)

Every word features a colored focal letter centered mathematically between the optical reticle guides. By locking your eyes on this point, your eyes never move across a page (no saccades).

2

Silence Subvocalization

At speeds above 350–450 WPM, your inner voice cannot pronounce words quickly enough. Your brain begins absorbing meaning directly from visual word forms.

3

Intuitive Gestures & Media Controls

Tap anywhere on the word display to pause. Double-tap the left side to rewind 10 words if you missed an idea. Use the scrubber bar to jump to any paragraph instantly.
