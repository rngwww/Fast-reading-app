## User & Collaboration Guidelines

- I am not a developer nor do I understand very technical terms. Always explain things simply, clearly, and avoid overly technical jargon.
- Always consult with me before implementing big changes. Ask for my opinion, and also share your own opinion and recommendations.
- After every big change, commit and push to this repository.
- Always use Git Bash (`"C:\Program Files\Git\bin\bash.exe"`) as the default terminal for executing commands instead of PowerShell (e.g., wrap commands with `& "C:\Program Files\Git\bin\bash.exe" -c "<command>"`).
